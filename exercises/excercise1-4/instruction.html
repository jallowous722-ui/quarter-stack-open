POWER IS BACK.
DON’T SWITCH EVERYTHING ON.

Do the steps in order. If the lights flicker or go off again, start over at step 1.

  1. 1

    Wait 5 minutes. Touch nothing.

    Power often returns, drops, then surges. Lights must stay steady for the full 5 minutes.

  2. 2

    Used a generator? Do this first.

    Turn off what it powers. Move the change-over switch to MAINS. Then stop the generator. Never leave both connected. No generator: go to step 3.

  3. 3

    STOPSmell, look, listen.

    Burning smell, sparks, buzzing, wet sockets or a tripped breaker? Leave the main switch OFF. Call an electrician.

  4. 4

    Main switch ON.

    Nothing else yet. Check that any light that comes on stays steady.

  5. 5

    Switch on one at a time, 1 minute apart.

    1. Fridge & freezerfirst
    2. Lightsthen
    3. Fans, water pumpthen
    4. AC, kettle, ironthen
    5. TV, computer, routerlast
  6. 6

    Fridge was off more than 4 hours?

    Throw away meat, fish, milk and cooked food. If unsure, throw it out.

Next time the power goes OFF:

Switch the main breaker off. Unplug TV, computer and router. Keep the fridge door shut.
